The Game of DOTS

The object of DOTS is to create boxes by drawing lines between the points. If you complete a box you receive a point and you get to go again.

Key : Function
HPulls up Help Screen.
EscEnds game.
EnterReady to draw line.
SSwitch players.

Arrow keys move marker until you press Enter. After pressing Enter, press an arrow key to draw a line on corresponding side of marker. You may also use `Diagonal' keys to move marker — Q E Z C, or 7 9 1 3 on the number pad.

On a touch screen, tap the gap between two dots to draw that line, or tap a box to move the marker there.
