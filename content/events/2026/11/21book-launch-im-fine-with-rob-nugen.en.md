Are you feeling disconnected, numb or unsure how to let anyone in?

When someone asks you how you are, do you say you're "fine" but deep
down you're not fine at all?

Do you find yourself struggling to ask or receive support but just
don't know how?

You're not alone and you don't need to carry this silent burden anymore.

I published this book for men however women have been wanting to
access this information too for themselves and to understand the weight
that men carry to bury their emotions in order to present a false sense
of masculinity that they can take it all.

If this speaks to you, I invite you to join me for my interactive
session to understand and work with your emotions without losing your
strength; break the lifelong patterns that keep you shut down and
distant; show up with honesty, courage and self-respect in your
relationships and lead your life confidently, aligned with the person
you know you can be.

#### This special book launch involves...

📖 **A Signed Copy:** Upon arrival or before departure, you will receive
my book, signed by me to continue your journey once this event is over.
You can pay this book forward by signing your name and pass it onto the
next person who's in need of it.

🙋‍♂️ **Opening Circle:** Check-in where you're at with yourself in a
non-judgemental space to connect with the group.

💁‍♂️ **Wellbeing Discussion:** A conversation around Chapter 7 from my
book, *How To Reframe Your Inner Critic*, we will explore how to deal
with negative self-talk. If you speak badly about yourself in ways you
would not talk about your friends, this process is for you. Find where
the message is coming from and give it a new helpful role in your life!

🛋️ **Optional Hot Seat:** One or more participants who consent to be
guided and supported through navigating their inner critic to gain
insight into a deeper self-awareness and a gentle way of healing a part
of themselves.

🙆‍♂️ **Closing Circle:** Check out, questions and wrapping things up.

This ticket includes a mini workshop session with Rob Nugen and a signed
copy of his book *I'M FINE!*

#### FAQ

**How do I get there?**
The venue is inside the Myer Centre in Rundle Mall, Adelaide. Take the
lift or escalators to level 2. It is an easy commute by tram, train or
bus, or the closest car park is Secure Parking - Rundle Place, Adelaide
Car Park.

**Do I need to bring anything?**
Pen & journal if you wish to write down notes or to help you gain
clarity. Outside food and drinks are not permitted inside Three of Cups
Teahouse, so beverages, treats and meals must be ordered at the venue to
support local business that supports local community.

**Who is this event for?**
Anyone who finds themselves comparing their self-worth to others and is
open to improving their relationship with self. This book is marketed
for men but women are welcome to attend as they will benefit from the
information presented as well.

**Do I need to participate in the opening and closing circle, discussion and hot seat?**
Opening circle yes, to form connection with the group; closing circle,
discussion and hot seat are optional. Rob will only take 1-2
participants for the hot seat to manage time.

**Does Three of Cups Teahouse offer gluten free, vegetarian or vegan options?**
Yes, from beverages, treats and toasties! Please order and pay at the
cash register and your items will be brought over to you.
