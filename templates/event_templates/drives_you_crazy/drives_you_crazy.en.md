# How to Stay With the Woman
## Who Drives You Crazy
### Though You Love Her So Much

**A free 90 minute live coaching session on Zoom. For men only.**

#### Does this sound like you?

* You feel guilty by default — like no matter what actually happened, you're the one who did something wrong.
* Her reactions feel unpredictable, and you've stopped trying to figure out the pattern.
* You've quietly stopped doing your own things, just to keep the peace.
* You're angrier than you used to be, and not totally sure at what.
* You've wondered if leaving is the only way out.

There are real reasons for all of this. And there's a way forward — one that
doesn't mean losing yourself, and doesn't necessarily mean
leaving.

#### What will we do?

* **Framing** (short): why the anger you're carrying is probably not only about her.
* **Live coaching**: I'll coach one or two men live, right there on the call. You watch. You notice what lands for you.
* **Reflection**: what did you see in yourself, watching?

#### What you'll leave with

No script — there isn't one for this. What you leave with is whatever showed up
for you watching another man look honestly at his own anger: maybe a boundary
you've been avoiding, maybe just relief that you're not the only one. And a
felt sense of whether working through this 1:1 is something you want.

#### Who this is for

Men in relationships with women they love — and who "drive them crazy." If
you've noticed yourself shrinking, managing her moods instead of relating to
her, or wondering whether leaving is the only way out, this is for you.
