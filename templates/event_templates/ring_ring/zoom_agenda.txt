A free 90 minute productivity experiment.  Men and women welcome.

First 25 minutes: Rob shares about "spiritual stuff" — consciousness, synchronicities, near-death experiences and past lives.  This part is recorded; the recording stops before the writing.

Then you write — guided prompts, some silence, optional music — listing whatever is blocking your progress.  Sharing in small groups follows.

You leave with two lists on paper: one easy action within 48 hours, and one thing you are willing to ask for help with.

You don't need to believe in anything spiritual; the exercise works the same either way.

Bring a notebook and pen.
