# Ring Ring!
## Your Life is Calling.
### Are You Willing to Answer?

**A free 90 minute experiment on Zoom. Men and women welcome.**

* You have lived many lives
* But right now you have just one life to live
* Synchronicities abound!

#### You are here because I went for a walk

One evening in August 2026 I attended a webinar by Carla Barbery and found a good deal of my own book looking back at me. I was inspired, so I went out walking. Because I was walking instead of lying down, I stayed awake for the podcast that came next.

In it, a man described how his two children had been killed in a tragic car accident; he went into a state of numbness for one year, and painted over all his windows so he couldn't see outside his house.

His children came to him in a vision and gave him the what for. "What are you doing? We've got work to do! Snap yourself out of it." No living human had been willing to say that to him... So his dead children decided to do it.

In the podcast, he said their message was the perfect wake up call and exactly what he needed to hear.  He called his former employer and went back to work the very next day.

🎧 [Hear that story yourself](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MlD3102w8Rw&t=4397s)

The next morning I posted my favourite slide from Carla's talk, with her permission. She reposted it and thanked me publicly.

A webinar rang. I answered it by going for a walk. The walk put me somewhere I could hear the actual call. And the answer to *that* one, it turns out, is a webinar.

Was that podcast my perfect wake up call??  I don't know yet.  Let's find out together.

#### The ninety minutes

* **The first 25 minutes** — near-death experiences, and why hundreds of strangers who have never met keep coming back with the same message. *This part is recorded.*
* **Then the recording stops.** Everything after that is just us.
* **The middle** — you write. Guided prompts, some silence, a little music. You will list what is weighing on you, then sort it into what you can control and what you cannot.
* **Sharing** — in pairs, or all together if we are a small group. Cameras welcome, never required.
* **The last stretch** — one easy thing you will do within 48 hours, and one thing you will ask for help with. Out loud, if you are willing.

#### What you will leave with

Two things on paper: one action you will take before the week is out, and one request for help that you have actually spoken.

#### Come as you are

I am going to say "God" a good deal. If that word costs you something, swap in whatever fits — the universe, your community, your future self. The exercise works exactly the same either way. You do not need to believe anything in particular in order to sort your own troubles into two piles and ask for a hand with the heavy ones.
