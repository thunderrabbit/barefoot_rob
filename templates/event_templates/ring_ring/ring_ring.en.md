# Ring Ring!
## Your Life is Calling.
### Are You Willing to Answer?

* You have lived many lives
* But right now you have just one life to live
* Synchronicities abound!

My second life may have started last night. Listening to a podcast, I heard about a man whose two children had been killed in a tragic car accident; he went into a state of numbness for one year, and painted over all his windows so he couldn't see outside his house.

His children came to him in a vision and gave him the what for. "What are you doing? We've got work to do! Snap yourself out of it." No living human had been willing to say that to him... So his dead children decided to do it.

In the podcast, he said their message was the perfect wake up call and exactly what he needed to hear.  He called his former employer and went back to work the very next day.

Was that podcast my perfect wake up call??  I don't know, but I'm going to run an experimental webinars a couple of times (or more!):  One in about a week after my inspiration and the next on the first weekend of September 2026 before I go to Australia 🦘🦘🦘 for 3 months with Jess.
