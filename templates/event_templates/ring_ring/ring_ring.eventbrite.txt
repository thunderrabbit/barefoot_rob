WHY THREE LINKS
=========================================================================

A recurring Eventbrite event has ONE description shared by every date.  There
is no per-date description to hide the right Zoom link in.  So every Zoom URL
in this listing has its date on the line directly above it, and all three are
present -- someone who booked the 31st can still find the 31st.

A single naked Zoom link anywhere in this listing is the bug: whoever booked
the other date follows it, registers for the wrong meeting, and shows up on the
wrong day.  If you shorten this copy, keep every link paired with its date.

If you ever split this into three separate Eventbrite events (one per session),
that constraint disappears -- each listing can then carry only its own link,
and the one under THIS SESSION'S SLOT above is the one to use.
