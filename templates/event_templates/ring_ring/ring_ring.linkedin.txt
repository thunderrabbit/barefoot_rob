Ring Ring! Your Life is Calling. Are You Willing to Answer?

I woke at 06:47, meditated for an hour, and the title of my new workshop came through. Here is what happened the night before.

On Monday 10th of August I attended a webinar by Carla Barbery and learned a lot about Burnout. With her permission, I posted my favorite slide from her talk.

Afterwards I realized I had been online all day, so I went for a walk in nature while listening to a podcast.

In the podcast, David Parker described how his two children had been killed in a tragic car accident; he became a shut-in for one year, even painted over all his windows so he couldn't see the world outside his house.

His deceased children came to him in a vision and chewed him out. "What do you think you are doing? We have so much work to do! ..." No living human had been willing to say that to him. So his dead children came to do it.

I burst into tears and bawled crying; this story is talking to me! "I'm so sorry; I'm so sorry I've been so scared to be seen. Please help me. Please help me serve."

I cried all the way home, cried in the shower, then had a good sleep. And woke up with this:

Ring Ring! Your Life is Calling. Are You Willing to Answer?

A free 90 minute productivity experiment on Zoom. Men and women welcome.

• The first 25 minutes: I share about "spiritual stuff" -- consciousness, synchronicities, near-death experiences, past lives. (This part is recorded; the recording stops before the writing.)
• The middle: you write. Guided prompts, some silence, optional music. You list whatever is blocking your progress.
• Then we share in small groups.

You leave with two lists on paper: one list of actions you can do, and one list of requests for which you need help.

You don't need to believe in anything spiritual. The exercise works exactly the same either way; we're just sorting our own troubles into two piles and asking for a hand with some of them.

Bring a notebook and pen.

Three sessions this month. Free.
