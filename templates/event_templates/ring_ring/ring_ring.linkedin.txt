After going to bed crying, I woke refreshed, and meditated for an hour during which I 'heard' the title of my new workshop:

"Ring Ring! Your Life is Calling. Are You Willing to Answer?"

Last week, Nathan told me our mutual friend Maxence would be at the Practical Philosophy Meetup starting at 7:00 p.m. I wanted to meet them both, but that meant skipping my online men's group meeting.

When I got to the location, the building was completely closed! I contacted Nathan and it turned out I was at the wrong building 🫨. Feeling frustrated, I asked a guard where the nearest train station was so I could head home.

I walked toward the station she pointed me to, and... there was Maxence!

We had a full 25 minutes to talk on the train while heading to the correct location together. After Practical Philosophy, we hung out for an impromptu men's group meeting because a couple other of my men's group friends were there.

By following my curiosity and apparently incorrect instructions, I got to meet Maxence *and* attend a men's group meeting, all on the same night. Amazing how things work out when I remain open to possibilities.

Ring Ring! Your Life is Calling. Are You Willing to Answer?

A free 90 minute productivity experiment on Zoom. Men and women welcome.

• The first 25 minutes: I share about "spiritual stuff": consciousness, synchronicities, near-death experiences, past lives. (This part is recorded; the recording stops before the writing.)
• The middle: you write. Guided prompts, some silence, optional music. You list whatever is blocking your progress.
• Then we share in small groups.

You leave with two lists on paper: one list of actions you can do, and one list of requests for which you need help.

You don't need to believe in anything spiritual. The exercise works exactly the same either way; we're just sorting our own troubles into two piles and asking for a hand with some of them.

Bring a notebook and pen.

I will run three free sessions this month.
