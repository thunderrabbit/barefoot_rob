# Ring Ring! Your Life is Calling — workshop content design

Companion to `2026-08-11-ring-ring-workshop-design.md`, which covers the
`generate_events.pl` scaffolding. This spec covers what the workshop *is*: its
argument, its run of show, its exercises, and what the event pages must say.

## The throughline

**Claim:** Help is available in abundance. It only arrives when asked for.
Asking is the hard part.

**Evidence:** the near-death-experience convergence — hundreds of independent
reports, no coordination between them, the same message: love is what matters,
life is meant to be hard, you are supported, ask when you need help.

**Enactment:** the two piles. The can't-control pile is not the shrug pile.
It is the ask pile.

**Takeaway:** every participant leaves having made one real ask, and having
named one easy action to take within 48 hours.

**Offer:** coaching as a structure for keeping the asking going. The pitch is
the content, not an interruption of it.

Every segment either builds the claim or enacts it. Anything that does neither
is material for a later session.

### The belief-neutral offramp

Said plainly in the first five minutes, once, without apologising for it
afterward: Rob will say "God" often; anyone for whom that word costs something
may substitute the universe, their community, their society, or their future
self. The exercise works identically. This is what keeps the skeptics in the
room, and the skeptics are who the workshop is for.

## The four prompts

Written verbatim before 19 Aug, because they get read aloud while watching
faces.

1. **Dump.** "List everything in your life that's weighing on you. Don't sort,
   don't judge, don't fix. Problems, worries, stuck things, unfinished things.
   Go for quantity."
2. **Two piles.** "Fresh page, line down the middle. Left: things you have some
   control over. Right: things you don't. If you're torn — could you take an
   action on it tomorrow, by yourself? If no, it goes right."
3. **Easy first.** "In the left column, circle the ones that are genuinely easy
   — you know exactly what to do and just haven't. Pick one. Write down when
   you'll do it, before Friday."
4. **The ask.** "Now the right column. This is the pile you cannot fix alone.
   That isn't a failure — that's the pile meant to be handed over. Pick the
   heaviest one. Write it as a request. Start with 'Please help me with…'
   Then say it out loud."

Prompt 4 is where the title pays off. "Your life is calling — are you willing
to answer?" The answer *is* the ask.

## Event page requirements

All three pages must answer, in the body:

- **What happens** — an agenda, in the style of the Mindful Sayonara page's
  minute-by-minute breakdown, not a bare "bring a notebook"
- **What you leave with** — one ask made, one 48-hour action chosen
- **Cost** — free, said explicitly. Silence reads as a catch
- **Who it's for** — men and women
- **Recording** — first 25 minutes only; recording stops before the writing
- **Time zone** — 14:00 / 19:00 **JST**, since Zoom is global. The 14:00 page
  should name a friendly overseas hour or two
- **Interactivity** — that there is silent writing, and sharing, and that
  cameras are welcome but optional
- **The other runs** — each page links its siblings, so someone who can't make
  one has somewhere else to go
- **The podcast source** — linked, so a curious participant can hear it

The "book a free discovery call" portrait already on the page becomes
on-message once the offer is coaching. Keep it.
